Create an app ShipTrackAdv/ (back-end & front-end), which offers services to track parcel shipments from pickup to delivery.

Deviate from the Regira layout, but preserve the functionality. Use a track & trace interface with a vertical status timeline, color-coded status badges and a shipment board grouped by current status.

Step 1
* Shipments can be fully managed.
* Shipments are collected at a pickup address and delivered to a delivery address by a Carrier.
* Shipments are routed through Hubs.
* Shipments have a Status and every status change is recorded as a timestamped Event with a Hub, a Reason and a remark.
* Carriers, Hubs, Statuses and Reasons can be fully managed.

Step 2
* Employees, Carriers and Customers authenticate.
* Roles and permissions determine what is visible: Administrators see everything, Dispatchers manage Shipments and routing, Hub Operators only register Events for their own Hub, Carriers only see Shipments assigned to them and Customers only see their own Shipments.
* Unauthorized Shipments must never be fetched, not through listings, filters, searches, exports or direct id lookups.
* Archived and cancelled Shipments can only be fetched by Administrators.
* Shipments follow configurable workflows: Statuses define which transitions are allowed, which ones are terminal and which ones require a Reason.
* Failed deliveries can be retried and Shipments can be rerouted or returned to sender.
* Every Event records who applied it.

Step 3
* Shipments can contain file attachments (proof of delivery, damage reports).
* Recipients can follow a Shipment on a public tracking page using its tracking code, showing a limited timeline without internal remarks.
* Estimated delivery dates are calculated and delayed Shipments are flagged automatically.
* Customers are notified automatically when the status of their Shipment changes.
* Display dashboards showing on-time performance, delays and hub throughput per Carrier.
* Generate delivery performance reports.
