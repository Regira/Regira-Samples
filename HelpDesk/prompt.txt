Create an app HelpDesk/ (back-end & front-end), which offers services to manage support tickets.

* Customers can create support tickets and assign one or more categories.
* Tickets have a priority, status and assigned employee.
* A ticket can contain multiple comments and file attachments.
* Administrators can manage categories, priorities, statuses and support teams.

Use a support center layout with Kanban boards, ticket queues and conversation-style comment threads.
