Create an app EventPlanner/ (back-end & front-end), which offers services to manage events and registrations.

* Events are organized at a location and can span multiple days.
* An event consists of multiple sessions, each with its own speakers and capacity.
* Employees can register for events and optionally select individual sessions.
* Administrators can manage locations, speakers, event categories and participant registrations.

Use a vibrant event-style interface with agenda views, speaker cards and prominent event banners.
