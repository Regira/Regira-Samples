Create an app ShopMate/ (back-end & front-end), which offers services to run a Shopping list.

* A shopper can manage multiple shopping lists.
* A shopping list contains articles that are sortable and can be activated (need to buy)/deactivated.
* An article can have multiple categories and shoppers can filter them easily, text search included.
* A category can have multiple parent/child categories.

Use a modern mobile-first design with large touch-friendly controls, swipe actions and a minimal interface.
